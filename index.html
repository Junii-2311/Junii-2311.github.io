Production AI

Systems I've shipped

Running here as simulations with sample data. Press an example and watch it work.

SYS_01RAG assistantBookme.pk

Bookie: an internal RAG assistant

Teams ask questions in plain language and get answers drawn from company content, with the sources shown. Built on BGE-M3 embeddings.

  • RAG
  • BGE-M3
  • Embeddings
  • Python
Under the hood

Documents are split into chunks and embedded with BGE-M3. A question is embedded the same way, the closest chunks are retrieved and handed to the LLM with an instruction to answer only from them and to cite them. If nothing relevant comes back, it says so instead of guessing.

SYS_02Predictive MLBookme.pk

Flight price direction & delay prediction

Classification models trained on 47,000+ historical flight records, with feature engineering and evaluation on accuracy, F1-score and confusion matrices.

  • scikit-learn
  • Pandas
  • Feature engineering
  • F1 / AUC
Under the hood

Each flight leg becomes a row of features (days to departure, weekday, recent fare movement, route and season signals). A classifier predicts whether the fare will rise, stay flat or drop, and is judged on held-out data with F1 and a confusion matrix, not just accuracy.

SYS_03LLM servingBookme.pk

Open-weight LLMs on our own GPUs

Gemma and Qwen served with vLLM on local GPU infrastructure, behind OpenAI-compatible REST APIs that internal AI apps call.

  • vLLM
  • Gemma
  • Qwen
  • Linux
  • REST APIs
Under the hood

vLLM batches concurrent requests on the GPU and streams tokens back as they are generated. Because the API is OpenAI-compatible, an internal app can switch between models by changing one field.

SYS_04AI automationIn daily use

AI scrum meeting automation

Records a stand-up, transcribes it, works out who spoke when, and has an LLM write a structured summary plus a personal update for each person.

  • Speech-to-text
  • Speaker diarization
  • LLM APIs
  • Linux
Under the hood

Audio goes through speech-to-text, then speaker diarization splits the transcript by voice. An LLM turns each person's lines into a structured update (done, next, blockers) with a fixed output schema.
